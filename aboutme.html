About Me

Hi, I'm the founder of We Plan Your Fun, your dedicated travel advisor. With years of experience in the travel industry, I help families and individuals create unforgettable vacation experiences.

My Journey into Travel

My passion for travel began early in life, sparked by family vacations and a curiosity about the world. What started as a hobby turned into a career when I realized how much joy comes from helping others discover new places and create lasting memories. As a travel agent, I get to combine my love for adventure with the satisfaction of making dreams come true for my clients.

I became a travel agent because I believe everyone deserves a vacation that rejuvenates and inspires. Planning trips isn't just about logistics—it's about crafting experiences that fit your lifestyle, budget, and dreams. Whether it's a magical Disney getaway or a relaxing cruise, I handle all the details so you can focus on enjoying the moment.

Independent Travel Agent with Mainstreet Travel

As an independent travel agent, I operate We Plan Your Fun as my own business, providing personalized service tailored to your needs. At the same time, I'm proud to be part of Mainstreet Travel Agency, a larger network of experienced travel professionals. This affiliation gives me access to a vast pool of knowledge and resources, ensuring I can answer any question and provide the best possible recommendations for your travels.

Being connected to this broad network means you benefit from collective expertise across destinations, airlines, hotels, and travel trends. No matter how complex or unique your travel plans, I have the support and know-how to make it happen seamlessly.
